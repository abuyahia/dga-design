# Executive Profile contract

Authority-owned profile for a configurable executive role. Required fields are
`title`, `description`, `name`, `role_label`, `portrait`, `portrait_alt`,
`biography`, and `updated`. Optional `qualifications`, `experience`, and
`related_links` remain declarative. The renderer validates product-local image
paths and safe internal or HTTPS links; it does not share or import the Ministry
Minister Profile.
